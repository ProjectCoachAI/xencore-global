Security

How your clients' data stays protected.

  • Isolated storage. Each customer's data lives in its own storage, encrypted with a key unique to that customer.
  • Ethical walls and clearance levels. A person walled off from a matter cannot see it, search it or be added to it, and the AI never uses its documents in their answers.
  • Two-step sign-in. The firm can require it for everyone.
  • Data residency. Files can be kept inside the United States or the European Union, as agreed with your team during the security review.
  • No training on your data. Your documents are never used to train AI models.
  • Deletion on request. Data is removed from the live system on request and from all backups within 30 days.
