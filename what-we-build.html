AI Conversation Data · In production

Diary — your AI conversations,
turned into data.

The answers you get from AI hold tables, figures, sources and next steps, and they usually disappear into a chat history. Diary keeps them, finds the data inside, and lets you search, compare and decide. It is the data side of our work: the same discipline we bring to ERP data, applied to AI conversations.
Capture
The Chrome extension saves a conversation from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral or Meta AI in one click. You can also import a conversation by pasting it.
Search by meaning
Find an entry by what it was about, not just the words in it. One search across every AI you use.
Tables and charts
Tables inside answers are detected automatically. Open one as a chart (bars, columns, heatmap and more) or export it to Excel or CSV.
Compare answers
When two AIs answered a similar question, Diary lines them up: figures both mention, figures only one mentions, and the sources each one cites.
Checklists and decisions
Next steps in an answer become a checklist and show up in My tasks. Add a decision note to any entry, so you know later what you decided and why.

AI Platform · In production

Forge — the decision
layer on top of AI.

Forge is our decision layer on top of AI, built by Xencore Global and used by professionals daily. Ask one question — eight AI engines answer simultaneously. The strongest answer surfaces instantly, backed by a consensus score across all responses. A decision engine, not just a chatbot.
Broadcast
Fire one question to all 8 AIs simultaneously. The strongest answer surfaces instantly — no side-by-side review required.
Perspectives
See all AI responses side by side with a live consensus score. Streamed in real time — ~30 seconds from question to synthesis.
Synthesis
Forge reads every AI response and produces one Best-of-Best answer. Multiple analysis modes: Executive Summary, Consensus Map, Divergence Spotlight.
Excel Analysis
Drop any .xlsx, .csv, or .tsv file. Ask a question. Eight AIs analyse your data simultaneously with automatic write-back fixes.
Continue from Diary
Open any conversation saved in Diary and carry on in Forge with several AIs at once. Every decision stays logged and searchable in Diary.
Forge Dock
Chrome extension that sits on every AI you already use. Switch providers, get perspectives, and access Forge without leaving the page.
